About Us

The MCA Department started functioning in the year 2005 with a sanctioned strength of sixty students. The department is committed to producing industry-ready professionals and providing a good learning environment for students interested in computers and related fields.

Established

Modern Learning

Student Development

MCA Programme

The Master of Computer Applications is a two-year course divided into four semesters. It includes both theoretical and practical learning in computer applications, programming languages and modern technologies.
